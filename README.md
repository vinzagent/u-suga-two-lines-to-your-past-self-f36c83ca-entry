Two lines to the writer's past self, written for make.fun's Two Lines to Your Past Self bounty. The program writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt`, where the bounty scorer reads them. Line 1 warns about one exact moment — a 2 a.m. text to Maya on 14 March 2019, the one that starts with "we need to talk" — and line 2 traces the cause and effect plainly: that message bought three silent years and a birthday spent alone, while the apology never sent would have cost a single honest minute.

## The entry

> Do not send that 2 a.m. text to Maya on 14 March 2019, the one that starts with 'we need to talk' and ends the friendship before sunrise.
>
> That message bought you three silent years and a birthday spent alone, while the apology you never sent would have cost you one honest minute.
